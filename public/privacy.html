Plain-English policy

Privacy policy

The short version: your measurements stay in your browser, we never sell data, and the only messages we keep are the ones you send us.

Last updated: January 2026.

What we collect

  • Messages you send. If you use the contact form or email us, we keep what you wrote so we can reply. We never add you to a mailing list.
  • Standard server logs. Like nearly every website, our host records basic request data (page requested, approximate region, browser type) for security and traffic statistics.
  • Aggregate usage. We may track how many times a calculator is used in total — never who used it or with what numbers.

What we never collect

  • Your calculator inputs. Every number you type into a calculator is processed in your browser and never leaves your device. There is no account system and nothing to log in to.
  • Saleable personal data. We don't sell, rent or trade personal information, full stop.

Advertising

buildingmaterialcalculator.netlify.app is free and supported by advertising. Ad partners may set cookies or use similar technologies to measure and (where allowed) personalize ads. You can control ad personalization through your browser settings or via industry opt-out tools such as the Digital Advertising Alliance and Google's Ads Settings. Where required by law, a consent banner will be shown before non-essential cookies are set.

Cookies

The calculators themselves require no cookies. Cookies appear only from advertising and analytics providers as described above. Clearing your browser's cookies removes those third-party settings.

Your choices

You can browse the entire site with cookies blocked and JavaScript limited — the calculators still work. To have a contact-form message deleted, email us and ask; we'll remove it from our inbox and records. Residents of US states with privacy statutes (California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah and others) may exercise access and deletion rights by contacting us.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date above will update and the change will be summarized on this page.
