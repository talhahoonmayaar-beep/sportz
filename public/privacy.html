Privacy policy

We explain what information this site processes, how contact messages are handled, and how third-party services may work if advertising or analytics are added.

Last updated: October 23, 2026.

What we collect

  • Contact messages. If you use our contact form or email us, we receive the details you submit, such as your name, email address, topic and message. We use this information to respond, maintain site operations and address reports. The form is handled through our hosting/form service, Netlify, and submissions may be stored by that service.
  • Server and security logs. Our hosting provider may process technical request data such as the requested page, IP address, browser/device information and timestamps for delivery, security and troubleshooting.
  • Calculator inputs. The calculator calculations are performed in your browser. The site is not designed to send the dimensions or material values you enter in a calculator to us.

What we never collect

  • Your calculator inputs. Every number you type into a calculator is processed in your browser and never leaves your device. There is no account system and nothing to log in to.
  • Saleable personal data. We don't sell, rent or trade personal information, full stop.

Advertising and third-party services

At the time this policy was updated, Google AdSense advertising code is not installed on this site. If advertising is added, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar identifiers to serve, measure or personalize ads, subject to applicable settings and consent requirements. We will update this policy when our services change. For information about Google's data use on partner sites, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use Google services.

Cookies and local storage

The calculator tools are designed to calculate values in your browser and do not require an account. Hosting, embedded features, or future analytics and advertising services may use cookies or similar technologies. You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings; blocking some technologies may affect certain site features.

How long information is kept and your choices

We keep contact messages only as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain records, or resolve a reported issue, subject to the hosting/form service's retention practices and applicable law. To request access to or deletion of a message you sent us, contact talhafarooq12malik@gmail.com. We will consider requests in accordance with applicable law. You may also manage cookies through your browser settings.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date and revise this page. Contact: talhafarooq12malik@gmail.com.
