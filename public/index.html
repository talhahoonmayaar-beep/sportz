Field note
Material quantities vary by product, compaction, moisture and local installation practice. Order slightly over your estimate when a partial load would stall the job.

Built for real project planning

These tools are designed around the questions homeowners actually ask a supplier: how much gravel do I need for a driveway?, how many bags of mulch fit a cubic yard?, will two gallons cover the bedroom? Instead of a bare number, each calculator page explains the calculation, lists typical coverage assumptions and flags the details that change real-world orders — compaction, irregular shapes, openers and closets, gate posts, pattern cuts.

Use estimates responsibly

Material quantities vary by product, compaction, waste, moisture and local installation practices. Calculators are planning tools, not engineering tools. For structural, electrical, gas, drainage or code-sensitive work, check manufacturer instructions and local requirements, and confirm quantities with your supplier before ordering a full load.
